# FAQ

## What age is PAN | DA for?
Primarily children aged 2 to 7. Content adapts to the chosen communication level — from non-verbal children to those already forming short sentences.

## Do we need to use the app together?
PAN | DA is designed mainly for practising together with a parent or therapist. Simple activities like Repeat can also be explored by the child at their own pace.

## Is PAN | DA only for pronunciation?
No. It focuses primarily on first words, vocabulary, comprehension, everyday phrases, and practising the same content in different ways. Speech sound practice (Pronunciation) is a separate feature.

## Does it replace a speech therapist?
No. PAN | DA is a home practice tool, not a diagnostic or therapeutic instrument. It works best alongside speech therapy — we recommend consulting your therapist on goals and progress.

## What is PAN | DA?
PAN | DA is an app for building vocabulary and communication in young children. Using picture flashcards, sounds and short activities, it helps children learn new words playfully — in all 9 supported languages.

## Who is the app for?
PAN | DA is for children learning to communicate — whether for first words, late talkers or extra speech support between therapy sessions (including children with speech delay or developmental dysphasia), or multilingual families. There is no strict age limit; the communication level setting matters more than age.

## Is PAN | DA suitable for children with developmental dysphasia or speech delay?
Yes. The app focuses on vocabulary, sentence building and comprehension — areas children with speech delay or developmental dysphasia often need to practise. For specific goals and guidance, we recommend consulting a speech and language therapist; PAN | DA complements that care, it doesn't replace it.

## How does Daily Practice work?
Every day PAN | DA picks a small set of words to practise. It remembers what your child has already seen and gradually brings words back for review. This helps you choose what to focus on during a short practice session.

## How do the training modes work?
PAN | DA offers 12 activities:
- **Repeat** — swipe through flashcards; every word is spoken aloud; tap syllables to practise pronunciation step by step (free)
- **Find** — the app says a word; your child taps the right image from 4 options; builds receptive vocabulary (Premium)
- **Guess the Sound** — identify animals and objects by their real sound, without hearing the name (Premium)
- **Phrases** — short sentences show how to use familiar words in everyday situations (Premium)
- **Matching Pairs** — classic matching game; the word is spoken when a matching pair is found (Premium)
- **Find in a Sentence** — hear a short sentence and find the matching picture; practise recognising a familiar word within a sentence (Premium)
- **Odd One Out** — which picture doesn't belong? Builds categorisation and logical thinking (Premium)
- **Speech Sounds** — practise specific sounds at the beginning, middle and end of words, and in short sentences (Premium)
- **What Is It For?** — hear a simple question about an object's purpose and choose the right picture; builds understanding of everyday object functions (Premium)
- **What Goes Together?** — your child connects two things that commonly belong together; builds logical and meaningful relationships (Premium)
- **Which Group?** — your child sorts familiar words into groups such as animals, food, vehicles and more; builds categorisation (Premium)
- **Follow Directions** — your child hears a two-step instruction and taps the pictures in the right order; practises listening and working memory (Premium)

## How do I unlock Premium modes?
Premium unlocks all modes at once. Try Premium free for 3 days from the App Store.

## How do I change the language?
In Settings, tap the flag or language name. App text and pronunciation change instantly — you can switch at any time in Settings.

## How many categories and cards are there?
Currently over 1,300 cards across 100+ categories: core words, food, animals, vehicles, nature, emotions, places, professions, manners, numbers, colours, shapes and more. Cards include short everyday phrases in all 9 supported languages.

## How do I add custom cards?
In Settings under **Family Cards** or **Classmates**, add photos from your camera roll. These categories are for practising the names of familiar people — photos sync privately via iCloud to your other devices.

## How do I hide a category or card?
In **Parental Settings** (protected section), use the **Packages** section to enable or disable entire categories. To hide individual cards, open the card list for a category — tap the eye icon next to any card.

## How does the communication level setting work?
When you set your child's level (non-verbal → full sentences), the app recommends categories and activities suited to their current stage, and adjusts the vocabulary shown in Daily Practice.

## What data does the app store?
Child settings, practice history and custom cards are stored on your device or synced through private iCloud storage. The current version also sends pseudonymous information about taps and app usage to PAN | DA's own server to improve the app. Photos and the child's name are not included in these statistics. See the Privacy Policy for details.

## Does the app work offline?
Yes. You can use the cards, sounds and spoken words without an internet connection. A connection is used for iCloud sync, purchases and sending pseudonymous usage statistics.

## Do you have tips for parents?
Try short, regular sessions and adjust their length to your child's interest and energy. Praise every attempt. Alternate between modes to keep things varied. Name pictures together and naturally use new words in everyday conversation.
